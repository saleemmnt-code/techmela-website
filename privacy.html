Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how TechMela handles your information.

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TechMela. We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly when you visit our website, contact us or place an order.

2. Information We May Collect

When you interact with TechMela, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact number.
  • Your email address, if you provide it.
  • Delivery address and order details when you place an order.
  • Messages and inquiries you send through WhatsApp or our contact form.
  • Basic website usage information, such as browser and device details.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your questions and inquiries.
  • Process and coordinate product orders.
  • Arrange delivery and provide order updates.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Address customer support requests.

4. WhatsApp Communication

TechMela provides WhatsApp links for customer inquiries and ordering. When you use these links, information you submit is shared through WhatsApp and handled according to WhatsApp's own privacy policy. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through chat.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information. Information may be shared when necessary with service providers, such as delivery partners, or where required by applicable law.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information shared with us. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and Website Data

Our website or its hosting services may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security and performance. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

8. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies before sharing personal information.

9. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request corrections, or ask us to stop using information where applicable law allows.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
